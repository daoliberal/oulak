Short answers to the questions I get most. If yours is missing, write to me.

How do flights work?

You choose a bird and write a letter. The server works out the flight from the real distance between your location and your friend's, and the bird flies it in real time. You can follow it on the map. Birds fly about 10 percent slower at night, except the owl, which is not slowed and only leaves after dark.

Why does a letter take so long?

On purpose. Birds fly slower than chat, so a letter is something you wait for. A pigeon flies about 70 km/h, a sparrow about 40 km/h and an owl about 55 km/h. A letter over 350 km takes a pigeon about five hours in daylight.

Can I send another letter while my bird is out?

Not with the same bird. A bird is busy from the moment it leaves until it is back home. You have three birds, so you can use another one.

How do I reply?

When a bird lands with a letter for you, it waits for your reply: 10 minutes for a pigeon, 5 for a sparrow and 25 for an owl. Open the letter and tap Reply. The same bird carries your reply home to the sender straight away. Once the bird has left, you can still write back, as a new letter with a bird of your choice.

Who can read my letter?

You can read it any time. The recipient can open it only after the bird has landed.

What do others see about me?

Your username, your city and area, and how far away you are in whole kilometres. Your exact point is never shown.

Which notifications will I get?

When a bird sets off to you, when it arrives with a letter for you, when your bird lands, and when it is home. Notifications never contain the text of a letter. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, then Privacy, then Delete account, and confirm. Your account, your letters (sent and received) and the Apple link are removed. It cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for details.
