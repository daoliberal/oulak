4. What Oulak is, and why it is slow

Oulak is for text letters between people who know each other. There are no photos or files. You can write only to people you add by username or invite. Each letter is carried by a bird over the real distance between you and the recipient, so delivery takes real time: minutes, hours, sometimes much longer. Some birds fly faster than others, some are slower at night, and some leave only after dark.

This slowness is the product, not a fault. For that reason:

  • I do not promise any delivery time. The times the app shows are estimates worked out from distance and bird speed. A letter can arrive later because of an outage, maintenance, a problem at Apple or another provider, your phone settings, or a poor connection.
  • Oulak is not for anything urgent or time critical, and it is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services.
  • A letter becomes readable to the recipient only after the bird has landed. The sender can always read it.

I may change, add or remove features, birds, wardrobe pieces, flight times and limits, and I may pause or end the service (see section 12 for what happens then). I will try to keep changes fair, and where a change takes away something you paid for, section 9 applies.

7. Reporting and blocking

You are in control of who writes to you.

  • My review. I review every report, and I aim to do so within 24 hours of receiving it. I act on what I find (section 8).
