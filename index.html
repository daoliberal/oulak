Letters that fly.

In Oulak, a bird carries every message. Pick a pigeon, a sparrow or an owl, write your letter, and watch it cross the map in real time. When the bird lands, it waits a little for a reply and carries it home.

How a letter travels

Birds fly slower than chat on purpose. A letter is something you wait for.

  1. Choose a bird and write

    Pick the bird that suits the letter, then write. Each bird carries a different amount of text.

  2. Watch it fly

    The bird crosses the real distance between you and your friend, live on the map. Only the city and the distance are shown.

  3. It lands, waits, and comes home

    The letter opens for your friend only once the bird has landed. The bird then waits a few minutes. If a reply comes in time, it carries it straight back to you.

Real distance, real daylight

Flight time comes from how far apart you really are. Birds fly a little slower at night, and the owl only sets off after dark.

Three birds

Every bird has its own pace, and you can give each one a name.

  • Pigeon

    Zeytin

    The dependable classic. Carries up to 500 characters.

    About 70 km/h. Waits 10 minutes for a reply.

  • Sparrow

    Fıstık

    Short notes, cheap and cheerful. Carries up to 100 characters.

    About 40 km/h. Waits 5 minutes for a reply.

  • Owl

    Gece

    Flies at night and leaves at dusk. Carries up to 300 characters.

    About 55 km/h, never slowed by night. Waits 25 minutes for a reply.

The handwritten names are what each bird is called to begin with.

Privacy in short

  • You sign in with Apple. Nothing else.
  • Your location is rounded to about 1 km. Other people see only your city and how far away you are.
  • A letter stays hidden from the recipient until the bird lands.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling data.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and your data goes with it.
