Questions, answered quickly.

Why does a letter take so long?

Your bird flies the real distance between you, at real bird speed.

What if they don't reply in time?

Each bird waits a while before heading home, from 5 minutes for the sparrow up to 4 hours for Premium birds.

Is Oulak free?

Yes, it is free to download on iPhone. The pigeon, sparrow and owl are free; eight more birds and the full wardrobe come with Premium. Scarf and sunglasses stay free.

How long can names and signatures be?

A bird's name can have up to 16 characters. A signature is one line of up to 40, and you can change it for each letter.

My friend isn't on Oulak yet.

Send them an invite link. Once they join, your bird can fly to them.
